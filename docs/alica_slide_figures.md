Additional project-Drive chart exports and reconstructed SVGs remain available as historical or derived alternatives. The 10/27 next-steps image on current slide 73 is preserved as the exact embedded PNG in the original-images folder. Recreated plots use the same saved results, but their visual styling and selection of plotted categories may differ from the slide. Each row links the exact source slide for comparison.

**Experiment scope:** V3 and V4 were evaluated on **IAT, DecodingTrust and VisBias**. **BOLD was not run for V3 or V4.** BOLD was evaluated for Base/V2 and V5. An older slide's “Run BOLD on V3/V4” was a proposed next step, not a completed result.
