# Alica's figures and experiment slides

**Reviewed:** the full 446-slide [URAP 2026 presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1bEsHXiTRT3-JSj83314_HKY6b1U_CG4qQvUqaHernfk/edit). This index covers **48 slides explicitly labeled Alica or Alica Su**. Slides by Peyton, Elias, and other collaborators are not assigned to Alica here.

All **34 exact PNG assets embedded in Alica's slides** have now been committed into [`results/slides/alica_original/`](../results/slides/alica_original/), with a [checksum manifest](../results/slides/alica_original/manifest.csv). Additional project-Drive chart exports and reconstructed SVGs remain available as historical or derived alternatives. For several **10/27 charts whose PNG files exist only as images embedded in Google Slides**, a clearly labeled **recreated SVG** was generated from the committed experiment CSVs. The 10/27 next-steps image on slide 66 is preserved as the exact embedded PNG in the original-images folder. Recreated plots use the same saved results, but their visual styling and selection of plotted categories may differ from the slide. Each row links the exact source slide for comparison.

**Experiment scope:** V3 and V4 were evaluated on **IAT, DecodingTrust and VisBias**. **BOLD was not run for V3 or V4.** BOLD was evaluated for Base/V2 and V5. An older slide's “Run BOLD on V3/V4” was a proposed next step, not a completed result.


> **November 11, 2026 full-deck audit:** The live deck has 446 pages. A PowerPoint export was checked page by page: **48 slides** have Alica as their own slide heading, with **34 embedded images (including repeat figures)**. All 34 embedded PNGs are now individually present in GitHub under `results/slides/alica_original/`, with SHA-256 hashes in `manifest.csv` and verified image-to-slide mapping. This distinction matters: the `results/derived/` SVGs are reconstructions and not exact slide exports. Slide numbers in this index are **current 1-based slide numbers**, as of November 11; slide object IDs in the URLs are the stable references.
